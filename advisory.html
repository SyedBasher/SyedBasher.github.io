Applied Work

Alongside academic research, I undertake selected policy and advisory projects involving economic analysis, quantitative research, forecasting, and sector or market questions. These assignments are usually organized around a specific analytical question.

Areas of work

  • Macroeconomic and financial analysis: inflation, monetary developments, credit conditions, banking, public debt, external accounts, and macro-financial risks.
  • Market and sector analysis: demand conditions, cost and price pass-through, market trends, scenario analysis, and quantitative support for business or sector questions.
  • Data, econometrics, and forecasting: time-series and panel analysis, forecasting, household and administrative data, measurement issues, and applied research design.
  • Policy research: research that connects evidence to a clearly defined policy question while making assumptions, data limitations, and uncertainty explicit.

Project-based work

I generally work on defined research or analytical assignments, independently or with an organization's internal team. The scope may range from a short analytical note to a larger research project or periodic advisory support.

For an initial discussion, a short description of the question, available data, and intended use of the analysis is sufficient.
