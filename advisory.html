HOW I WORK

Question first, method second.

  1. Define the decision or question.What exactly needs to be understood, compared, forecast, or tested?
  2. Audit the evidence.Identify the data, measurement limitations, assumptions, and relevant comparison points.
  3. Choose the analytical method.Use the simplest method that can answer the question credibly, and more sophisticated methods only where they add value.
  4. Communicate the result.Present the economics, uncertainty, and implications in language appropriate for decision-makers.
