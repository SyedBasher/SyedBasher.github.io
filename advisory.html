Policy & advisory

Applied and advisory work

Alongside academic research, I undertake selected projects involving economic analysis, policy research, forecasting, and quantitative work. These assignments are usually organized around a specific question rather than a standard consulting product.

Areas of work

Examples of questions where my research background and applied experience may be useful include the following.

Macroeconomic and financial analysis

Inflation, monetary developments, credit conditions, banking, public debt, external accounts, and macro-financial risks.

Market and sector analysis

Demand conditions, cost and price pass-through, market trends, scenario analysis, and quantitative support for business or sector questions.

Data, econometrics, and forecasting

Time-series and panel analysis, forecasting, household and administrative data, measurement issues, and applied research design.

Policy research

Research that connects evidence to a clearly defined policy question while making assumptions, data limitations, and uncertainty explicit.

Engagements

Project-based work

I generally work on defined research or analytical assignments, either independently or in collaboration with an organization's internal team. The scope can range from a short analytical note to a larger research project or periodic advisory support.

For an initial discussion, a short description of the question, available data, and intended use of the analysis is sufficient.
