About

I received my Ph.D. in Economics from York University. My career has included academic research and teaching, central banking, policy research, and consulting in Bangladesh and Qatar. I currently work independently and undertake selected analytical projects through Gold River Analytics, with much of my recent research focused on Bangladesh.

Current work

My current work combines academic research with applied economic analysis. Recent projects examine household welfare and prices, macro-financial conditions, public debt, regional economic measurement, export opportunities, and the use of administrative and remotely sensed data where conventional economic statistics are limited.
