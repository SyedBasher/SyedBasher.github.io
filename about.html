About

I am an economist with experience in academic research, central banking, teaching, policy work, and independent research. I received my Ph.D. in Economics from York University in Canada and have worked in Bangladesh and Qatar.

Background

From 2007 to 2013, I worked as a Research Economist at Qatar Central Bank. During part of that period, I also worked as an economic consultant for the Qatar National Food Security Programme. I later served as a Senior Research Fellow at Fikra Research & Policy.

In Bangladesh, I joined East West University, where I later became Professor of Economics and served as Chair of the Department of Economics. I remained at the university until 2025.

My research has covered macroeconomics, development, financial markets, energy economics, regional economics, and applied econometrics. More recently, much of my work has focused on Bangladesh and on combining conventional economic data with household surveys, administrative records, financial information, and remotely sensed data.

I currently work as an independent researcher and undertake selected applied and advisory projects.
