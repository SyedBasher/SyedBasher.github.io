ABOUT

Economist, researcher, teacher, and former central-bank economist.

I earned my Ph.D. in Economics from York University, Canada. My career has moved between academic research, central banking, policy work, teaching, and independent research, with experience in both Bangladesh and Qatar.

BIOGRAPHY

Research shaped by different institutional settings.

From 2007 to 2013, I worked as a Research Economist at Qatar Central Bank, alongside consulting work for the Qatar National Food Security Programme from 2010 to 2012. I later served as a Senior Research Fellow at Fikra Research & Policy.

In Bangladesh, I joined East West University and later became Professor of Economics, serving there until 2025. My research has covered macroeconomics, financial markets, energy economics, development, and applied econometrics. More recently, I have concentrated on economic questions where Bangladesh's conventional data can be combined with household surveys, financial information, and remotely sensed data.

I am currently an independent researcher. I also write regularly for a wider audience on economic and policy questions.
