Research

My research is in applied economics. Recent work focuses on Bangladesh, regional and spatial economics, household welfare, macro-financial questions, economic measurement, energy economics, and forecasting.

Research interests

  • Bangladesh economy and development
  • Regional and spatial economics
  • Macroeconomics and finance
  • Household welfare and prices
  • Energy and climate economics
  • Applied econometrics and forecasting
