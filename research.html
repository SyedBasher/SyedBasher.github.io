RESEARCH

Applied economics across development, macro-finance, regional data, and energy.

My recent work increasingly uses Bangladesh as a setting for questions about growth, spatial inequality, household welfare, debt, credit, and economic measurement. I also continue a longer research program in energy economics, financial markets, and time-series econometrics.
