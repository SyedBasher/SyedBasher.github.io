Syed Abul Basher

Economist and Independent Researcher

I am an economist and independent researcher based in Dhaka. I previously served as Professor of Economics at East West University and as a Research Economist at Qatar Central Bank. My work combines academic research with applied economic analysis, using household surveys, financial and administrative data, and remotely sensed data.
