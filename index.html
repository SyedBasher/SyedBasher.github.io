Syed Abul Basher

Economist and Independent Researcher

My research is in applied economics, with particular interests in Bangladesh, regional development, macroeconomics and finance, household welfare, energy economics, and applied econometrics. Much of my recent work uses household surveys, financial and administrative data, and remotely sensed data to study economic questions for which conventional data are limited.
