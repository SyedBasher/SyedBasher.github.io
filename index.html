My current research focuses mainly on economic questions related to Bangladesh: regional disparity, household welfare and prices, the energy sector, and the building of economy-wide models, both computable general equilibrium and macroeconometric. The empirical work draws on official statistics as well as household surveys, administrative records and satellite images. For many years I have also worked on oil prices, exchange rates, food security and the economies of the Gulf.

Mangrove Intelligence

Mangrove Intelligence is the company I founded in 2026 to build data and analytical products on the Bangladesh economy: databases, indicators and regular forecasts that are kept up to date rather than delivered once and filed away. It also takes on economy-wide modelling and analysis for organisations that need it.
