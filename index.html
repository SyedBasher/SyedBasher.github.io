CURRENT WORK

Four connected research areas

I work across academic and policy questions, with Bangladesh as an increasingly important empirical setting.

01

Bangladesh economy & development

Growth, employment, inflation, external debt, structural transformation, and the institutions that shape development outcomes.

02

Regional economics & remote sensing

District-level income, spatial inequality, convergence, night lights, and methods for measuring local economic activity where conventional data are limited.

03

Household welfare & prices

Demand systems, price shocks, household expenditure, welfare measurement, and the distribution of economic pressure across households.

04

Energy, climate & financial markets

Energy prices, clean-energy equities, climate risk, asset pricing, forecasting, and financial-market responses to shocks.
