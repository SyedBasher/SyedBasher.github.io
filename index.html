Areas of work

Research and policy interests

My work spans academic research and applied analysis. These areas overlap rather than sit in separate compartments.

01

Bangladesh economy & development

Growth, employment, inflation, external debt, structural transformation, banking, and the institutions that shape economic outcomes.

02

Regional economics & remote sensing

District-level income, spatial inequality, convergence, night lights, and local economic measurement where conventional data are limited.

03

Household welfare & prices

Demand systems, price shocks, household expenditure, welfare measurement, and how economic pressures differ across households.

04

Energy, climate & financial markets

Energy prices, clean-energy equities, climate risk, forecasting, and financial-market responses to economic shocks.
