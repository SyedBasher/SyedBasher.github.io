Applied Work

This page records selected commissioned and institutional assignments in economic analysis and applied research. The emphasis is on the analytical question, the evidence used, and the type of work undertaken.

Selected projects

2026 · Bangladesh Institute of Development Studies

A macroeconometric model of Bangladesh

The assignment concerns the construction of a macroeconometric framework for Bangladesh. The work brings together macroeconomic time series and estimated relationships so that alternative macroeconomic developments and policy scenarios can be examined in a coherent setting.

2025–2026 · Economic Research Group

Household indebtedness and financial vulnerability within the MFI framework in Bangladesh

This project studies household and microfinance-sector indebtedness using branch and institution-level administrative records. The analysis focuses on borrowing intensity, repayment stress, portfolio quality, and indicators that can help identify areas where financial vulnerability warrants closer investigation.

2025 · Change Initiative

Political Economy of Debt in Sri Lanka and Bangladesh

A comparative research assignment on public debt and its institutional and political-economy setting in Sri Lanka and Bangladesh. The work examines how financing structures, policy choices, and institutional constraints shape debt outcomes and the policy space available to governments.

2022 · Asian Development Bank

Bangladesh Flash Floods 2022: Post-Disaster Needs Assessment

Contributed economic analysis to the post-disaster assessment following the 2022 flash floods. The assignment formed part of a broader effort to document losses, recovery needs, and the economic dimensions of the disaster.

2022 · KIVU International

New sectors diversification in Bangladesh's exports

The assignment examined opportunities for export diversification beyond established sectors. The work considered sector and product evidence relevant to identifying areas with potential for market expansion and further analytical screening.

2019 · Institute of Water Modelling · World Bank financed

A hydro-economic model of the Jamuna River

Joint work with the Institute of Water Modelling on a hydro-economic framework for the Jamuna River. The project connected water-system conditions with economic considerations so that changes in the river system could be assessed alongside their economic effects.

2018–2019 · Bangladesh Bank, Sustainable Finance Department

Monetary and exchange-rate research

Empirical research on monetary and exchange-rate questions in Bangladesh, supported by the Sustainable Finance Department of Bangladesh Bank. The work used macroeconomic data and econometric analysis to examine policy-relevant relationships.

2010–2012 · Qatar National Food Security Programme

Economic Consultant

Worked on economic questions related to food security and agricultural policy in Qatar. The role connected quantitative economic analysis with programme design and the constraints facing food production and food-security planning in a small, import-dependent economy.

Areas of work

  • Macroeconomic and financial analysis: inflation, monetary developments, credit conditions, banking, public debt, external accounts, and macro-financial risk.
  • Econometrics and forecasting: macroeconomic modelling, time-series and panel methods, forecasting, and quantitative research design.
  • Household and microeconomic analysis: household surveys, indebtedness, financial vulnerability, demand, prices, and welfare.
  • Regional and spatial analysis: subnational economic measurement, geospatial data, satellite night lights, and regional disparities.
